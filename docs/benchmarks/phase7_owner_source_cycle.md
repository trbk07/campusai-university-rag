# Personal owner source benchmark — r8

Author and reviewer: **Trương Bảo Kiên**, as explicitly requested by the owner.
Approval is the owner's standing chat instruction; separate per-question forms
are unnecessary for this personal profile.

The original 180 records are human-authored per the owner's declaration. Their
prior candidate exposure is proven by exact split hashes and recorded qids; all
remain in dev. The fresh 120 records were generated by Codex under the owner's
instruction and retain `author_type=ai` and `owner_directed_ai_generation`.
Owner review and AI generation are disclosed in the benchmark profile.

Fresh questions cover course credits, two-course sums and absent course codes.
Positive facts require actual course/code/credit headers in frozen table bytes.
Every fact binds its full table row digest, page and chunk. Negative codes must
be absent from the complete indexed corpus. Two disjoint course facts form one
family, including its easy, hard and negative variants. Thirty families divide
into test/holdout without sharing source facts; no inference output was used
to choose questions or splits.

## Reproduction

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m scripts.benchmarks.create_owner_source_benchmark `
  --destination data/benchmark/phase7_review_cycles/<new-cycle>

# Copy the five frozen Phase 6 M0 reports into a new results directory.
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m evaluation.reranker.release_workflow human-freeze `
  --benchmark-dir data/benchmark/phase7_review_cycles/<new-cycle> `
  --results-dir evaluation/results/<new-cycle>
```

The generator refuses an existing destination and history overlap. Repeated
measurement of a frozen cycle preserves its bytes. The held-out bank must stay
unchanged after quality is observed.
